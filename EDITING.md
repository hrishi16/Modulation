# Editing Modulation

Go to the site address with `/admin/` on the end, log in, and you'll see **Site** with nine sections. Open one, change it, press **Publish → Publish now**. The live site updates in about a minute; reload to see it.

## What each section controls

**⚙︎ Settings, theme & menu** — the site name, the banner along the top (switch it off at launch), the theme (dark or light background, the accent colour, the heading typeface), the menu names and which sections are switched on, and the footer.

**Home page** — the opening text, the felt ↔ measured dial, the quotation and the film. Each dial pair is an artwork plus a science image; add more pairs and the arrows appear by themselves.

**The exhibition** — the six panels. Every image has a *What kind of image is this?* choice: *Artwork*, *Science image*, or *Art made on a science image*. The site labels images from that choice, so choose honestly — a micrograph the artist has drawn on is the third kind, never the second.

**Take part** — the images people choose between in the body-map interactive, the three words for each, and the science line shown after choosing. The mechanism itself is fixed; its content is yours.

**Invited artists** — one entry per artist, each with its own room and menu tab. Sections can be a plain row of images, or a *book with magnifying lens* like Khushbu's diary. For Manika: switch off *Work still to come* and add a section when her work arrives. For a new artist: **Add artists**, give a name and a one-word web address (e.g. `manika`), switch on *Show in menu*.

**Workshops** — every workshop with its poster, description, prompts and a strip of participant work. Only publish participant work with consent to publish; fill *Made by* only when the maker asked to be named.

**Lineage** — the museum links and the essay.

**Contributors** — people, roles, short bios.

**Extra pages** — makes a new tab. Build a page from blocks: text, heading, image, gallery, quotation, prompt, links, YouTube video. *About the project* is an example.

## Good habits

- **Pictures:** under about 1 MB each and around 1500 px on the long side. Phone photos straight off the camera are too big — resize first.
- **Order:** drag the ≡ handle on any item to reorder panels, workshops, artists, images.
- **Bold and italic** in the larger text boxes use the toolbar. In smaller boxes, `*italic*` and `**bold**` work.
- **Nothing is lost.** Every Publish is kept as a version and can be restored.

## What needs a developer

Everything above is editable without code. A new *kind* of interaction — something that behaves unlike the dial, the body map, the magnifying lens or the image viewer — or a full redesign, needs someone to write code once. After that, its content is editable here like everything else.

## Showing it to Asmita — about 15 minutes

1. **The live site (3 min).** Open the site address. Walk the tabs: dial on Home, a panel on The exhibition (click the Pain in the Brain painting — art and science open side by side), Take part, Diary Pages with the lens, Workshops with the posters and participant strip.
2. **The editor (2 min).** Open `/admin/` in a second tab and log in. Point out that it's just forms — no code anywhere.
3. **A text edit (2 min).** *Home page* → change a word in the opening paragraph → **Publish → Publish now**. Switch to the site tab, wait a minute, reload.
4. **A new workshop (4 min).** *Workshops* → **Add workshops** → a title, a date, upload any poster image → Publish. Reload the site — it's there, with the poster clickable.
5. **The look (2 min).** *Settings* → Theme → change the accent colour or switch to Light → Publish → reload. Then switch it back.
6. **A new tab (2 min).** *Extra pages* → open *About the project* → switch *Show in menu* off and on, or add a text block → Publish.
7. **Close with the ceiling:** content, images, new artists, workshops, pages, theme — all hers. New interactions — a developer, once.

Do the demo edits on the real site; undo them afterwards (or restore from GitHub's version history).
